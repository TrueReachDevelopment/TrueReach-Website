The Chef
Marco
Deluca
Executive Chef · Marea, since 2018

Chef Marco spent a decade cooking in Michelin-starred kitchens across Italy and South Florida before bringing his philosophy of "honest ingredients, fearless technique" to Tampa Bay. His menus honor Florida's coastal bounty while drawing on classical European tradition. Under his direction, Marea has become one of Tampa Bay's most celebrated dining destinations.

Nightly Ritual
Watch the Sun
Set Over the Bay

Every table in our dining room faces Tampa Bay. Our open-air terrace is positioned perfectly — arrive at 7:00 and you'll watch the sun descend into the water while your first course is served.

Terrace seating available by request · First-come on walk-ins

Live Music
Jazz Every
Friday & Saturday

Our resident quartet performs from 7 – 10 PM every Friday and Saturday evening. Classic jazz, bossa nova, and contemporary standards fill the dining room. Arrive early for seats closest to the stage.
